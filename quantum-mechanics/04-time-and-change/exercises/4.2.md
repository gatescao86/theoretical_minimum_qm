## Problem

Prove that if $M$ and $L$ are both Hermitian, $i[M, L]$ is also Hermitian. Note that the $i$ is important. The commutator is, by itself, not Hermitian.

## Solution
We know that 
$$
(ML)^\dagger = L^\dagger M^\dagger \\
(M+L)^\dagger = M^\dagger + L^\dagger
$$
$$
(i[M, L])^\dagger = i^*(ML-LM)^\dagger \\
= -i[(ML)^\dagger - (LM)^\dagger]
= -i( L^\dagger M^\dagger -  M^\dagger L^\dagger)
$$
Since $M=M^\dagger$ and $L=L^\dagger$
$$
-i( L^\dagger M^\dagger -  M^\dagger L^\dagger) = -i(LM-ML) = -i[L,M] = i[M,L]
$$
Therefore, $i[M, L]$ is Hermitian.
