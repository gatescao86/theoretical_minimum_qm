## Problem

Prove that if $U$ is unitary, and if $|A \rangle$ and $|B \rangle$ are any two state-vectors, then the inner product of $U |A \rangle$ and $U |B \rangle$ is the same as the inner product of $|A \rangle$ and $|B \rangle$. One could call this the conservation of overlaps. It expresses the fact that the logical relation between states is preserved with time.

## Solution
For $U |A \rangle$, the corresponding bra is  $\langle A|U^\dagger$

The inner product of  $U |A \rangle$ and $U |B \rangle$ is therefore
$$
\langle A|U^\dagger U|B \rangle
$$
Since $U$ is unitary, i.e. $U^\dagger U = I$
$$
\langle A|U^\dagger U|B \rangle = \langle A|B \rangle
$$
