## Problem

Using the inner-product axioms from §1.9:

**(a)** Using the axiom for innner products, prove:

$$
\{\langle A|+ \langle B|\} |C\rangle = \langle A|C\rangle + \langle B|C\rangle
$$

**(b)** Show that $\langle A|A\rangle$ is a real number.

## Solution

### (a)
```math
\begin{aligned}
\{\langle A| + \langle B|\} |C\rangle
&= [\langle C| \{|A\rangle + |B\rangle\}]^* \\
&= [\langle C|A\rangle + \langle C|B\rangle]^* \\
&= \langle C|A\rangle^* + \langle C|B\rangle^* \\
&= \langle A|C\rangle + \langle B|C\rangle
\end{aligned}
```

### (b)
$$\langle A|A\rangle = \langle A|A\rangle^*$$
Since any complex number equal to its own complex conjugate must have zero imaginary part, 
$\langle A|A\rangle$ is real.
