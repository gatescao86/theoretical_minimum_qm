## Problem

Prove that Eq. 3.16 is the unique solution to Eqs. 3.14 and 3.15. 


## Solution
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 
(\sigma_z)_{11} & (\sigma_z)_{12} \\
(\sigma_z)_{21} & (\sigma_z)_{22}
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix} 
1 \\
0
\end{pmatrix} = 
\begin{pmatrix} 
(\sigma_z)_{11} \\
(\sigma_z)_{21}
\end{pmatrix} = 
\begin{pmatrix} 
1 \\
0
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 
(\sigma_z)_{11} & (\sigma_z)_{12} \\
(\sigma_z)_{21} & (\sigma_z)_{22}
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix} 
0 \\
1
\end{pmatrix} = 
\begin{pmatrix} 
(\sigma_z)_{12} \\
(\sigma_z)_{22}
\end{pmatrix} = 
-\begin{pmatrix} 
0 \\
1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Therefore, 

$$
\sigma_z = 
\begin{pmatrix} 
1 & 0 \\
0 & -1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
