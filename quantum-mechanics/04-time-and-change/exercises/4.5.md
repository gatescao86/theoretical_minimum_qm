## Problem

Take any unit 3-vector $\vec{n}$ and form the operator

$$
H = \frac{\hbar \omega}{2} \sigma \cdot \vec{n}
$$

Find the energy eigenvalues and eigenvectors by solving the time-independent Schrödinger equation. Recall that Eq. 3.23 gives $\sigma \cdot \vec{n}$ in component form.

$$
\sigma_n = 
\begin{pmatrix} 
n_z & (n_x - in_y) \\
(n_x + in_y) & -n_z
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Solution

From Lecture 3, we know that $\vec{\sigma} \cdot \vec{n}$ has eigenvalues $\pm 1$. Multiplying it by $\frac{\hbar \omega}{2}$ only rescales them, so the eigenvalues are 

$$
E = \pm \frac{\hbar \omega}{2}
$$

And the eigenvectors are 

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 
cos \frac{\theta}{2} \\
e^{i \phi} sin \frac{\theta}{2}
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 
-e^{i \phi} sin \frac{\theta}{2} \\
cos \frac{\theta}{2} 
\end{pmatrix}
$$
