# Exercise 3.4

**Section.** 3.7

## Problem

Let $n_z = cos \theta, n_x = sin \theta cos\phi,$ and $n_y = sin \theta sin \phi$. Angles $\theta$ and $\phi$ are defined according to the usual conventions for spherical coordinates. Compute the eigenvalues and eigenvectors for the matrix.

## Solution
$$
\sigma_n = 
\begin{pmatrix} 
cos \theta & sin \theta cos\phi - isin \theta sin \phi \\
sin \theta cos\phi + isin \theta sin \phi & -cos \theta
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\det(\sigma_n - \lambda I) = 0
$$
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 
cos \theta - \lambda & sin \theta cos\phi - isin \theta sin \phi \\
sin \theta cos\phi + isin \theta sin \phi & -cos \theta - \lambda
\end{pmatrix}
$$
$$
\det(\sigma_n - \lambda I) = (cos \theta - \lambda)(-cos \theta - \lambda) - (sin \theta cos\phi - isin \theta sin \phi)(sin \theta cos\phi + isin \theta sin \phi) = 0
\\
\lambda^2 = sin^2 \theta + cos^2 \theta
$$
$$
\lambda = \pm 1
$$

Then, for each eigenvalue $\lambda$, find the eigenvector by solving 
$$
(\sigma_n - \lambda I)
\begin{pmatrix} 
x \\
y
\end{pmatrix} = 
\begin{pmatrix} 
0 \\
0
\end{pmatrix}
$$
For $\lambda = 1$, 
$$
\begin{pmatrix} 
cos \theta - 1 & sin \theta cos\phi - isin \theta sin \phi \\
sin \theta cos\phi + isin \theta sin \phi & -cos \theta - 1
\end{pmatrix} 
\begin{pmatrix} 
x \\
y
\end{pmatrix} = 
\begin{pmatrix} 
0 \\
0
\end{pmatrix}
$$
We know that $e^{i\phi} = cos \phi + isin \phi$ and $e^{-i\phi} = cos \phi - isin \phi$
$$
(cos \theta - 1) \cdot x + e^{-i\phi} sin \theta \cdot y = 0
$$
$$
\frac{y}{x} = e^{-i \phi}\frac{1- cos \theta}{sin \theta} = e^{i \phi} tan \frac{\theta}{2}
$$
Let $x = cos \frac{\theta}{2}$, then $y = e^{i \phi} sin \frac{\theta}{2}$

The eigenvector is $\begin{pmatrix} 
cos \frac{\theta}{2} \\
e^{i \phi} sin \frac{\theta}{2}
\end{pmatrix} $

Similarly, for $\lambda = -1$, The eigenvector is $
\begin{pmatrix} 
-e^{i \phi} sin \frac{\theta}{2} \\
cos \frac{\theta}{2} 
\end{pmatrix} $
